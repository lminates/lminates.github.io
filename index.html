NINE TO FREE STUDIO

Nine to Free Studio is a private production tool for the Nine to Free YouTube channel, which makes educational videos about money habits, mindset and time.

What the app does
  • It runs only on the channel owner's own computer.
  • After the owner approves a script, it creates the video and uploads it to the owner's own YouTube channel (youtube.upload).
  • It shows the owner the statistics of their own channel and videos (youtube.readonly).
It has no other users, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.
